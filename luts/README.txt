LUTs for ReefHue
================

Place .cube LUT files here (3D, 1D, or 1D shaper + 3D). ReefHue lists them in the
"LUT & Presets" tab; "Import…" copies a file into this folder (or into your
personal LUT folder if this one is read-only).

Bundled looks (made for ReefHue, free to use): ReefHue Warm Reef, Clear Blue,
Film Fade, Punchy. They can be regenerated with tools/make_starter_content.py.

Technical LUTs (e.g. Log -> Rec.709 from your camera maker) should be set to
"Apply first" in the LUT position.
